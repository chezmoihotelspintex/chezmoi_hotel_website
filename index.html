About Chez Moi

A hotel located at Spintex, in Accra, and about 15 minutes drive away from Kotoka International Airport. Chez Moi Hotel is arguably the most affordable hotel at Spintex, a prime location in the heart of Accra. At Chez Moi, we offer comfort, in a serene environment, and ensure that we cater to the needs of guest, so that when our guests check out, they check out feeling satisfied. The hotel happens to be located in the same community as some of Ghana's largest & finest shopping mall chains - China Mall, Palace Mall, Max Mart, Orca Deco, Melcom, etc. Some of the finest restaurants also happens to be within close proximity. Therefore, providing a convenient stay for guest that like to go shopping. For the food lovers, Chez Moi also happens to find itself in the same cummunity as some of Ghana's best restaurants, serving local & continental. And if you are a night life lover, there are a number of pubs and night clubs within distance to chill out for the night.

"There is only one boss - the guest."
